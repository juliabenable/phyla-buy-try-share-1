Showing the $55 on the Opportunity page

Eight ways to make the $55 more prominent: five stickers on the photo, then three in the way Airbnb shows a price (bold number in a plain text line, or a white pill on the photo). Stickers on the photo

The Airbnb way

Airbnb never uses a sticker for price. It sets the number in bold with the unit in regular weight next to it ("$120 night"), shows a discount as a struck-through price beside the new one, and keeps photo badges to a small white pill ("Guest favorite").

The footer next to "I'm in" keeps "$55 value · Fully reimbursed, tax included" in every option. Airbnb patterns are described from my knowledge of its listing pages, not from a fresh capture. Sticker shapes are drawn in SVG, so they stay sharp on any screen.
