The deal in one paragraph

Rising creators (200 to 999 followers, posting skincare) buy Phyla Acne Phage Serum ($55) at Target, in store or online, upload their receipt and video, post one feed post and one story, and get the serum plus tax paid back. No brand approval and no Benable review. Pilot: 25 creators, then 100 minimum. Phyla pays $19 per creator plus reimbursements; billing stays off the portal.

Recruiting

  • New Dashboard group "Buy, Try, Share", wired like the existing ABC flows. Adding creators to it keeps them out of the standard onboarding messages.
  • Logan's list of about 300 creators. Katie adds the copy to the group; first send planned for Nov 25 around 10am.
  • They go through the normal Brand Portal onboarding (terms included).
  • These creators stay out of regular collabs.

Rules

  • Progress: 4 segments, no labels, one countdown for the whole flow.
  • Deadline: 10 days to post, shipping time included.
  • Payout: triggered when both posts are live, not by a date. Amount = the serum plus its tax from the receipt, sent through the existing PayPal / Venmo payouts.
  • Usage rights: in perpetuity, stated in the creator brief and terms. No extra UI.

Brand Portal

  • Its own "Buy, Try, Share" item in the left nav, separate from Campaigns. Same table design as the Rights tab.
  • Header: title, Active pill, one-line description, See brief (PDF; the only actions are download or email Katie).
  • Four cards, no progress bars: Accepted, Bought at Target, Posted (each "x of 25" with a small %), and Your pilot (25 creators, with a link to ask for more, which emails Katie).
  • Filters: All, Posted, Bought, Accepted, with counts.
  • Table columns, in order: Creator (photo, name, handle; no follower count), Bought at (store name and city, or target.com), Bought on, Total paid (serum plus tax), Status (Accepted / Bought / Posted with a short note), Reel and story (two thumbnails).
  • Clicking the reel thumbnail opens the post on Instagram in a new tab. The story thumbnail is shown but does nothing on click. No in-portal viewer (Julia, Nov 25).
  • Empty state, before any creator accepts: the three cards read 0 of 25, and the filters are hidden and the table is replaced by "No creators yet", one sentence and a "See brief" button. See it.
  • Not shown to the brand: receipt photos, reimbursed status, returns checks, amount owed.
